## Analysis

The **Prelude in C Major** (BWV 846) is one of the most famous keyboard works by [Johann Sebastian Bach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Sebastian_Bach). It serves as the opening movement of *The Well-Tempered Clavier, Book I*.

### Structure

The piece is built almost entirely on arpeggiated chords.

```abc

T:Theme (Arpeggio Pattern)

z2 E2 G2 c2 e2 G2 c2 e2 | z2 F2 A2 d2 f2 A2 d2 f2 |
```

It explores various harmonic progressions before arriving at the final C Major chord.
